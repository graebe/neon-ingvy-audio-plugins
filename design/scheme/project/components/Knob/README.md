# Knob

The default control for any continuous parameter. A `bg-200` disc, a 270° `line-200` rail with its gap at the bottom, a `uv` value arc and a 2px pointer; `label` above, `Readout` below. The arc runs from the minimum to the value. A bipolar parameter (pan, detune, a delay from −100 % to +100 %) draws its arc from 12 o'clock to the value instead, to the left for a negative value and to the right for a positive one, so at the centre value there is no arc at all, only the pointer at 12. Consumer provides: label, value 0–1, formatted readout string, size (`knob` 48 default, `knob-lg` 64 at most once per window), bipolar, detents, disabled, and the info strings of the knob and its readout. Never draw a knob without its readout: the arc shows position, the readout shows the number.

**Detents.** A knob may hold on values that mean something musically: the Trance Gate's Length holds at ½, 1, 2 and 4 bars for the current Rate and meter. Each detent is a 1px radial tick just outside the rail (from radius 22 to 24 on the 48px knob), in `ink-dim` like every inactive mark; the tick at the current value is `uv`, without glow. A drag holds on a detent for about 14px of travel, from either side, and is no slower anywhere else; a drag that starts on a detent leaves it after half that. Shift (fine) ignores the detents, because a fine drag is for the values between them. Page Up and Page Down jump to the next detent, and by a tenth of the range where there is none that way. Typed values, the double-click reset and the arrow keys are untouched by detents, and so are the parameter, its automation and the host: a detent is a feel in the editor only. Keep them to musical landmarks; a knob is not a stepped switch.

Interaction: drag vertically (200px for the range, Shift five times finer), double-click resets to the default, one click on the readout types into it, and from the keyboard arrows step, Page Up/Down take large steps or detents, Home/End go to the ends and Enter types (Interaction conventions). Keyboard focus is `glow-focus` on the disc.
