# RadialMenu

A circle of an object's controls, opened around the click that selected it, so the controls come to the object instead of the pointer travelling to a column. Use it where a window edits many small objects in one well (notes, regions, points) and each object has the same handful of parameters.

A `bg-100` plate (radius 84px) on a `line-200` hairline holds up to eight items on a ring of radius 56px, the first at 12 o'clock and the rest clockwise. A **value item** is a knob's dial at its own size (disc 16, rail 20, the value arc in `uv`, detent ticks just outside the rail); a **bipolar** item lights its arc from 12 o'clock both ways, like a bipolar knob. An **action item** is a disc with its glyph in `ink` (product glyphs allowed, as in a tool column). Each item's label sits outside the plate on a `bg-100` chip; the centre carries what the menu acts on ("3 notes", "All") or, while an item is pointed at or dragged, that item's value with its unit. The item under the pointer rises to `bg-300`.

Value items drag as knobs do: up and down, 200px for the range, Shift five times finer, detents held for about 14px, 4px of dead zone, one edit per drag, double-click resets. An item may hand Shift to a second parameter instead of fine control (a length behind a shape); its hint says so. Action items act on a click; a drag that left the dead zone never clicks. A press anywhere outside the plate and its labels closes the menu and still does what it does there. To the keyboard the menu is one stop: Tab or Left and Right move between items, Up and Down change a value (1 %, Shift 0.2 %), Enter acts, Escape closes. Consumer provides: the items (id, glyph, label, kind, value 0–1, bipolar, detents, value text, enabled, Shift as second parameter and its value), the centre text, where it opens and the bounds it must stay inside, and what a drag, a reset, an action and closing do. The menu always opens inside the window.
