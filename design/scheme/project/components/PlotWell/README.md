# PlotWell

The frame of a plot the engine renders: an envelope, the gate across one cycle, a signal before and after the plugin. A `bg-000` well with a `line-100` hairline, its content inset 6px all round, and a caption in 10px `hint` style, uppercase, `ink-muted`, top left in a 14px band above the content. The caption names the picture and its span in clauses three spaces apart ("SIGNAL   ONE CYCLE, 500 MS   DRY IN GREY, GATED IN FRONT"). Draw what the engine renders, never a second model of the DSP rebuilt from the parameters: a plot that reasons about the sound is a second implementation, and it drifts from the first.

Inside the well, data colours only (Colour): a curve is a 2px `uv` line with its arc glow, the area under it `plot-fill`; a reference curve behind it (the envelope as dialled, behind what the gate leaves of it) is a 1px `plot-ghost` line; the dry input behind a processed trace is `plot-dry` at half opacity, the processed trace `uv`. A position mark (a step edge, where the gate closes) is a `line-200` rule, `amber` only when something runs past it, and it is the window's one amber mark. A millisecond ruler runs along the bottom in `line-200` with 3px ticks and `hint` labels about 38px apart; a landmark value (the step's length) carries its unit and the other ticks give way to it. Size the well to the content it lines up with (the Trance Gate's band is the pads' 760px); the view box is the pixel size, never stretched, so captions keep their shape. Consumer provides: width, height, caption, the rendered data, the info string.
