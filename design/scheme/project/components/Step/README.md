# Step

One cell of a sequencer: a 40px square well. `off` is `bg-200`; `on` is a solid `uv` fill with `glow-led`; a partial amount (`on partial`) keeps the `bg-200` well, takes a `uv` border and fills with `uv` from the bottom, the lit height being the amount; `tie` carries the step before it on through this one, so it is not a fill: a hollow `uv` outline (the hairline doubled inward to 2px) with a 2px `uv` bar across its middle, on the `bg-200` well, with no glow because it does not trigger; `play` (the playhead) adds `glow-led` and, on an off step, a `uv-glow` wash; `accent` is an `amber` border. States combine (`on play`, `tie play`). Consumer provides: on, tie, amount 0–1, playing, accent, an optional index for the first step of each beat. Click toggles (an on step or a tie goes off), Shift-click turns an on step into a tie and a tie back on, vertical drag sets the amount, and a step switched on comes back at its full amount; from the keyboard, Space or Enter toggles (Shift: tie) and Alt with Up or Down sets the amount. State the conventions in the window's `Hint` ("click a step to toggle – shift-click for a tie – drag up or down for its amount").
